# AI Provider Compliance Audit

Audit date: 2026-02-10

Starwoven sends user intentions through 5 commercial AI APIs and presents responses under oracle persona names. This document tracks compliance with each provider's terms of service and acceptable use policies.

## Summary

| Provider  | Entertainment | Persona Names                    | Per-Response Labels | General Disclosure   | Commercial | Data Training   |
| --------- | ------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------- | -------------------- | ---------- | --------------- |
| OpenAI    | Allowed       | Allowed (with disclosure)        | Not required        | Required             | Allowed    | No (API)        |
| Anthropic | Allowed       | Allowed (with disclosure)        | Not required        | Required             | Allowed    | No (API)        |
| Google    | Allowed       | Allowed (jurisdiction-dependent) | Not required        | Required             | Allowed    | Paid tier: No   |
| DeepSeek  | Allowed       | Allowed (mandatory disclosure)   | Not required        | Mandatory & explicit | Allowed    | May train       |
| xAI       | Allowed       | Allowed (minimal disclosure)     | Not required        | Minimal              | Allowed    | No (Enterprise) |

## Starwoven's Compliance Measures

- **General disclaimer** on the home page explicitly states AI-generated responses
- **About page** (`/about`) explains how the system works, names all oracle personas, and discloses that all responses are AI-generated
- **No per-response "AI-generated" labels** — not required by any provider
- **Persona names** (Iris, Luna, Echo, Shade, Nova) are compliant — all providers allow creative presentation with general disclosure

## Provider Details

### OpenAI (GPT-4.1 — Iris)

- **Terms reviewed:** Usage Policies, Services Agreement, Sharing & Publication Policy
- **Entertainment use:** Permitted without restriction
- **Disclosure:** Content must indicate AI involvement in a way "no reader could possibly miss." General disclaimer satisfies this.
- **Data training:** API data is NOT used for training by default
- **Status:** Compliant

### Anthropic (Claude Sonnet 4.5 — Luna / Claude Opus 4.6 — Starweaver / Claude Haiku — moderation)

- **Terms reviewed:** Usage Policy (Sept 2025 update), API Terms, Privacy Policy
- **Entertainment use:** Permitted, explicitly allowed for consumer-facing applications
- **Disclosure:** Organizations must help users understand they're interacting with AI. General disclosure sufficient.
- **Data training:** Commercial API data NOT used for training
- **Status:** Compliant

### Google (Gemini 3 Pro Preview — Echo)

- **Terms reviewed:** Generative AI Terms, API Terms of Service, Usage Policies
- **Entertainment use:** Permitted and encouraged
- **Disclosure:** Required, jurisdiction-dependent. NY state law requires disclosure for synthetic avatars but has "expressive work" exception.
- **Data training:** Paid API tier: no. Free tier: data may be used for improvement.
- **Action required:** Confirm paid API tier is in use
- **Status:** Compliant (assuming paid tier)

### DeepSeek (DeepSeek Reasoner — Shade)

- **Terms reviewed:** Open Platform Terms of Service, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy
- **Entertainment use:** Permitted
- **Disclosure:** MANDATORY — "If you publish or disseminate outputs, you must clearly indicate the output content is generated by artificial intelligence." Stronger requirement than other providers.
- **Data training:** DeepSeek reserves the right to use inputs AND outputs to "provide, maintain, operate, develop or improve" services. This is the most permissive data policy among our providers.
- **Risks:**
  - User intentions (potentially sensitive) may be used for model training
  - No opt-out mechanism documented for API data
- **Status:** Compliant on disclosure (via About page). Data training risk remains.

### xAI (Grok 4.1 — Nova)

- **Terms reviewed:** Acceptable Use Policy, Terms of Service (Consumer & Enterprise)
- **Entertainment use:** Permitted and designed for it (Grok has built-in entertainment personas)
- **Disclosure:** Minimal — general attribution requested, not per-response
- **Data training:** Enterprise API data NOT used for training
- **Competitive restriction:** Cannot develop "models or services that compete with xAI." Starwoven is entertainment, not competing.
- **Status:** Compliant
